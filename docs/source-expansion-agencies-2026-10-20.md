# Agency and foundation source expansion — second wave (20 October 2026)

This pass compares candidates with `data/source_registry.json` and [the first expansion pass](source-expansion-2026-10-20.md). It excludes the already-listed EUD/EEAS, EBRD, EIB, World Bank Group/IFC, IMF and GIZ. Sources below are first-party. “Presence” includes active country operations where the agency does not maintain a separately documented local office; those are marked as weaker leads. A global career system is not evidence that the agency is currently recruiting in BiH.

## Screening outcome

Only **KfW** is a clear additional agency with both an explicitly listed Sarajevo office and a first-party staff careers channel. **CEB** and **AFD** are useful watchlist sources for project-related or regional posts, but this check did not establish BiH-duty-station jobs; ADA has no verified local office yet, and SDC/SECO evidence resolves to embassy hiring. There are not five to eight strong non-duplicate leads in this institutional segment once “active projects” and “a global jobs page” are kept distinct from local employer recruitment.

No source-registry or code files were changed. This is research only; all vacancy-status statements above are a point-in-time check on 20 October 2026.
