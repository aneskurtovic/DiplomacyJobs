## Additional improvements and context

- Ludo's simulated-user seeding reports nine name conflicts and only 63 of 72 roster members available. Resolve seed-name collisions without overwriting real user accounts; make the roster completeness check visible.
- Woodpecker has repeated `stream: not found` and workflow lease/queue errors, including events before disk exhaustion. Investigate separately rather than attributing every CI error to storage. Hooks filtered out by `when` conditions are not automatically failed builds.
- Alloy's predominant errors are inspection races for deleted containers during deployment. Its historical dropped batches were rejected for old timestamps (July/August), not evidence of current ingestion loss. Check checkpoint persistence and distinguish expected removal races from actual delivery failures.
- Caddy's dominant warnings are unsolicited ACME challenge requests (mostly a crawler), plus canceled CI event streams and game WebSockets. These do not by themselves establish a certificate failure. Short backend connection refusals align with the observed Ludo deployment around October 29 09:33 UTC.
- The host journal occupies 2.3 GB and is dominated by firewall blocks. Review journal retention and firewall log rate limits while retaining useful security evidence. Repeated SSH authentication/protocol failures are visible; this audit does not establish a compromise.
- Old Apport logs show failure to collect containerized .NET crash reports because `/usr/share/dotnet/dotnet` does not exist on the host. Improve container-specific crash diagnostics if those reports are needed; the latest such retained record is October 8.
- Most other DiplomacyJobs upstream errors recovered: Spain 8/86 failed attempts, Mostar 4/84, Pakistan 3/84, and scattered timeouts/HTTP errors elsewhere. A retry policy should be bounded, source-aware, and preserve the 24-hour UNDP procurement limit.
- Scrape health currently uses a 48-hour stale-success threshold. NATO's newer consecutive failures are therefore absent from the health response. Add a separate consecutive-failure signal to expose regressions sooner without making an isolated network timeout an outage.
