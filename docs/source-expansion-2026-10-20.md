## Screening notes

- **Excluded as already represented:** the registry already contains EUD/EEAS, EBRD, OSCE, UN agencies (including UNICEF, UNDP, IOM, UNFPA, UN Women and UNHCR), RCC, OHR, Council of Europe, World Bank Group, Save the Children, CRS, UNOPS, EIB, GIZ and others. These were not counted as additional leads.
- **Danish Refugee Council:** not recommended as a new BiH source. DRC's own 2023 annual report says it planned to close its Bosnia and Herzegovina operation by 2025; older BiH vacancies and current DRC global openings therefore do not establish a current BiH hiring channel.[DRC annual report](https://job.drc.ngo/media/govozt1e/annual-report-2023_drc_final-08-05-2024.pdf)
- **Mercy Corps:** not recommended despite a legacy BiH location appearing in its careers alert form; first-party evidence found in this pass concerned old programming and did not verify a current BiH operation or current BiH opening. The alert form alone is insufficient evidence of current presence.[Careers alert form](https://jobs.jobvite.com/careers/mercycorps/jobAlerts)
- **Current opening claims are deliberately narrow:** CARE's paid expert call had closed (27 September), FES's internship call remained open to 31 October but did not disclose compensation, and World Vision's official Workday feed had two open regional roles allowing BiH home working. The other listed channels did not show a verified open BiH staff post in the sources reviewed. Keep channel inclusion separate from live vacancy status.

## CARE Balkans and Hilfswerk International: source-level feasibility

### CARE Balkans

- **Best staff-vacancy source:** the official [Jobs and Tenders page](https://care-balkan.org/jobs-and-tenders/) has a distinct `Jobs` section. At check time it showed one `Job Announcement – FINANCE OFFICER`, dated 16 April 2026. The employer's full notice says it was full-time in Prishtina and the application deadline was 30 April 2026, so it was neither BiH-based nor open on 20 October.[Notice](https://care-balkan.org/2026/04/16/job-announcement-finance-officer/)
- **Feed and completeness:** the site exposes the public WordPress REST API at `https://care-balkan.org/wp-json/wp/v2/posts`; it returns `X-WP-Total` and `X-WP-TotalPages` headers and supports `per_page=100&page=N`. On 20 October the unfiltered archive had 385 posts over 4 pages. Category ID `385` is `Jobs` and has one post; category ID `381` is `Tenders` and has two. Therefore the existing `wordpress` adapter is a fit for staff vacancies with `categories: [385]` and `allow_empty: true`; its paging checks can establish complete enumeration of that category for the target year. Suggested record URL: `https://care-balkan.org/wp-json/wp/v2/posts` (no query string in the source URL; adapter adds paging and field parameters).
- **Do not treat the jobs category as the complete paid-individual-work feed.** The September 17, 2026 expert call is categorized `Announcements` (411) and `News` (3), rather than `Jobs`; a direct notice specified a financial offer, part-time October–November work, BiH eligibility, and a 27 September deadline, now expired.[Expert call](https://care-balkan.org/2026/09/17/call-for-experts-ewi-regional-innovation-fund/) To track expert/consultant contracts as well, scan all posts (385 across 4 pages at time checked) or the relevant `Announcements` category, then filter individual-consultant vocabulary and inspect the linked notice. That broader stream includes ordinary project announcements and calls for beneficiaries, so title/category matching alone is not enough to label a post as a paid job. CARE's current [Jobs and Tenders page](https://care-balkan.org/jobs-and-tenders/) and its 2026 dated notices showed **no open, BiH-eligible staff vacancy or paid expert call on 20 October 2026**.
- **HTML fallback:** job list entries are article cards with title/date links and category labels; it shows a short latest-items selection, while the REST API is preferable for complete coverage and has explicit completeness headers. Keep `Jobs` and `Tenders` separate.

### Hilfswerk International BiH

- **Best vacancy source:** the official [Jobs and Careers category](https://hwi.ba/en/news?category_id=32) is a dedicated, first-party listing. As of 20 October 2026 it displayed five entries: two full-time Sarajevo job vacancies dated 21 March 2025, two expert calls dated 18 February 2025, and a Migration Management Coordinator hiring notice dated 2 July 2024.[Category listing](https://hwi.ba/en/news?category_id=32) The two March 2025 job notices had application deadlines of 28 March 2025.[Assistant](https://hwi.ba/en/news/job-vacancy-assistant-fundraising-and-proposal-development/123), [Coordinator](https://hwi.ba/en/news/job-vacancy-coordinator-fundraising-and-proposal-development/124)
- **Completeness and selectors:** this is a server-rendered HTML site, not WordPress; no RSS or public JSON endpoint was found. On listing pages the cards use `div.article-box.article-box-news`, with a child `a[href]`, `time[datetime]` and `h2.article-title`; these provide detail URL, ISO posting date and title. Category ID `32` is the displayed “Jobs and Careers” filter. The first page contains all five category items; `?category_id=32&page=2` returns the explicit “There are currently no news items in this category” empty state. Thus a `generic` adapter can use listing URL `https://hwi.ba/en/news?category_id=32`, the card link selector, `allow_empty: true`, and `intermediates: true`; configure/verify extraction of listing dates if the generic adapter relies on detail-page metadata. Page 2 should be treated as the pagination/empty check, not as another source.
- **Open status:** the category has no 2026 item, and the newest staff notices expired in March 2025; there is **no open 2026 BiH job or paid individual call** on the official Jobs and Careers listing as checked. No new official vacancy should be inferred from the site's active general news stream. A focused category crawl is more complete and lower-noise than crawling all news.
