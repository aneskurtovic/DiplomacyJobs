## Screening and exclusions

- All five organization names were absent from the registry and the first expansion note at the time of comparison. CARE International Balkans, World Vision BiH, Hilfswerk International, Helvetas, People in Need, Pro Peace, Swisscontact and FES are already discussed in the first note and were excluded.
- Existing registry entries include Save the Children, Catholic Relief Services, CARE, and RYCO; they are not repeated here. Caritas Switzerland is distinct from Catholic Relief Services.
- SOS Children's Villages BiH currently lists two paid roles with notices that say they are open until filled; neither supplies a closing date. The latest dated individual call verified in this wave is ASB's February 2026 paid consultancy, now closed. Caritas's verified hiring example closed in 2025. JRS's general CV invitation is not a dated vacancy and is not proof of a paid role.
- **Suggested order:** SOS Children’s Villages BiH; ASB SEE; Caritas Switzerland in BiH; WWF Adria (regional-only watchlist); JRS BiH (manual watchlist pending a specific compensated call).
