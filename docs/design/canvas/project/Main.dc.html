A calmer, deadline-first, evidence-led job board

An audit of the public site, a token-based design system that keeps today's institutional blue, and redesigned job list, job page and sources screens. Phase 1 (the launch fixes) is already in the code.

123 → 0 raw hex colours in site.css, now all design tokens
16 → 7 font sizes in the proposed type scale
2.5 → 3.66 input border contrast; 3:1 is the WCAG minimum
+35px → 0 sideways overflow on phones, at 320–390px

Design principles

Ranked: when two conflict, the lower number wins.

01

Evidence beside every claim

Each extracted fact can show the sentence it came from. Every count says when it was checked. Unknown stays "—", never 0.

Trade-off: pages carry more small print; disclosure keeps it one tap away instead of removing it.

02

Deadline first

"Can I still apply?" comes first. The deadline is the second-strongest element on a card, and the only one coloured for urgency.

Trade-off: employer and tags get less weight; the title still leads.

03

Calm on a phone

The first screen shows results. Secondary filters sit behind one disclosure, and Apply stays within thumb reach.

Trade-off: requirement filters take one extra tap on phones.

04

One meaning per colour

Blue is brand and links. Green means covered, red means ends today, amber ends soon, violet is in review and grey is not tracked. Colour always comes with a label.

Trade-off: no decorative colour; brand expression comes from type and the seal.
