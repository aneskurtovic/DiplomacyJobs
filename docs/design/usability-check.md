# Mobile usability check (Design Phase 3, item 6)

A moderated check with five job seekers on their own phones, from the [design proposal](design-system.html) Rollout section. It needs real participants, so it has not been run; this is the script for it. Run it on the deployed site (or a phone on the same network as the dev server), in Bosnian unless the participant prefers English.

## Participants

Five people who could realistically apply for these jobs: at least two recent graduates, at least one person over 40, at least one on Android and one on iPhone. None should have seen the site before. Five sessions usually surface most of the serious problems; more sessions mostly repeat them.

## Set-up (5 minutes)

- Ask permission to take notes and, if they agree, to record the screen. No names in the notes.
- Say: "We are testing the site, not you. Please think aloud. There are no wrong answers."
- Start from a blank browser tab. Give them the address, not a link.

## Tasks

Note for each task: success without help, success with a hint, or failure; time taken; where they hesitated; what they said.

1. **"Find a job you would qualify for in Sarajevo."** Watch whether they find the City select, open *Filteri*, use education or experience, and understand the chips. Ask afterwards: "How sure are you that you qualify? What told you?"
2. **"You want to apply before the deadline. How much time do you have, and where do you apply?"** Watch whether they read the deadline column and countdown, open the job page, and use *Službeni oglas* or the sticky bar. They should not submit anything.
3. **"Is this site official? Would you trust it?"** Watch whether they notice the trust strip, the "putem …" label on syndicated jobs, or go to *Izvori*.
4. **"Find out whether this site watches the job page of the Embassy of Japan."** Watch whether they find *Izvori*, use the cards, the bar or the name search, and understand "Bez potvrđenih oglasa".
5. **"Send this job to a friend on Viber or WhatsApp."** Watch how they share and whether the preview shows the share image (title, employer, deadline).

## Wrap-up questions (5 minutes)

- What was the hardest part?
- What would make you come back? Would you follow new jobs (feed) or prefer e-mail?
- Light or dark mode on your phone? Did anything look wrong?

## After the sessions

List each problem with how many of the five hit it and its severity (0–4, as in the audit section of the proposal). Fix anything that three or more people hit before adding features. Record the results and date in HANDOFF.
