# Candidate discovery and account rollout

## Product decision

"Ads" here means the portal's verified job postings. People can always browse the full public board. `/for-you/` lets an anonymous visitor enter a few preferences and see ranked jobs immediately; the choices stay in their session. Registration is optional and saves a private profile. No CV or nationality is requested. Work eligibility is an optional yes/no/unsure answer.

Matching uses only currently visible, published jobs and deterministic rules in `board/matching.py`. A title, preferred location or remote work, opportunity type, field, education, experience and languages can improve rank. A job is excluded from the personalized list only when it clearly conflicts with a city, remote-work or opportunity-type requirement the person explicitly marked mandatory, or they marked that job "not relevant." Unstated employer requirements remain visible as a **possible match** with an unknown-requirement flag. Education and experience normally explain or caution; they do not silently exclude. Every card links to the official application and has match details. The regular search remains available even when no personalized jobs match.

## Release sequence

1. **Anonymous discovery:** Deploy migration 0043 and the `/for-you/` page. Keep all three switches at `0`. Review a sample of current real ads in Bosnian and English, including unclear requirements, expired ads and no-match cases. Confirm the verified-source and duplicate rules still hold. The preview must work without email or an account.
2. **Accounts:** Set up a real transactional email service and sender domain, then test activation and password recovery through actual mailboxes and both languages. Add a resend path for lost activation mail and a retention process for inactive, unconfirmed accounts before opening signup. Complete the candidate privacy notice with the actual operator, contact, provider, hosting/transfer facts and retention policy; have the operator review it against applicable law. Set `CANDIDATE_OPERATOR_NAME`, `CANDIDATE_PRIVACY_EMAIL`, `CANDIDATE_EMAIL_PROCESSOR`, `DJANGO_EMAIL_*`, and `CANDIDATE_MAIL_ENABLED=1`. Only then set `CANDIDATE_SIGNUP_ENABLED=1`. The registration endpoint checks these settings and otherwise returns 404. Test signup, confirmation, login, reset, editing, opt-in changes and deletion in production before inviting users.
3. **Small pilot:** Invite the first relevant candidates through existing professional contacts, university career centers and useful search pages, without implying employer affiliation or complete vacancy coverage. Ask for "not relevant" feedback and missing-employer reports. Review the match cards and source coverage weekly. Keep both optional email choices visible but dormant; signup does not subscribe someone by default.
4. **Job alerts:** Only after a pilot shows useful matches and a dependable flow of new jobs, set `JOB_ALERTS_ACTIVE_SINCE` to that launch date and `JOB_ALERTS_ENABLED=1`. The weekly command defaults to a read-only dry run. Inspect its candidate/job counts before scheduling `candidate_digests --send` once a week. It sends at most five new, uncautioned matches per opted-in person, never an empty digest or the same job twice. It reserves each weekly attempt before sending; a failed or uncertain attempt is marked `needs_review` and needs manual inspection, not automatic replay. Unsubscribe is in every digest.
5. **Product news:** The separate opt-in is recorded, but there is no campaign sender yet. Do not send product news or reinterpret alert consent as newsletter consent. Build the sender, unsubscribe path, suppression rules and approval workflow only when there is a concrete campaign.

No email scheduler or production mail configuration is part of the initial deployment. Account activation and password recovery become possible only after the account-mail switch and service are configured. Digest delivery needs its own separate switch and explicit `--send` command.

## Measures and review

`DiscoveryMetric` stores daily aggregate counts for personalized visits, returns, no-match visits, official clickouts and "not relevant" feedback, without visitor IDs or profile content. Compare clickouts to discovery visits and inspect no-match and feedback rates by current job supply. These counts are directional: the clickout route can be visited directly and a session is only a rough return measure. Do not call them applications or hires. Review actual user comments alongside counts, and fix mismatches before expanding outreach or enabling alerts.

## Operations

Local checks: `python manage.py check`, `python manage.py makemigrations --check --dry-run`, and `python manage.py test board.test_candidates`. Production deploy runs migrations; no existing candidate rows need conversion. Do not enable signup until the account gates above are complete. Keep `JOB_ALERTS_ENABLED=0` until the later alert gate. Run `python manage.py candidate_digests` to preview; `python manage.py candidate_digests --send` is the deliberate delivery action and refuses to run without the alert, mail and base-URL settings. Monitor `CandidateDigest` in admin for `needs_review`; investigate before any manual retry. Deleting a candidate account removes its profile, feedback and delivery history. Personalized pages use private, no-store responses and the service worker skips candidate routes.
