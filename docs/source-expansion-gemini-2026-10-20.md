# Screening the proposed embassy, agency and INGO sources

Checked **20 October 2026** against first-party pages and `data/source_registry.json`. This note focuses on leads not already assessed in the [mission audit](source-audit-2026-10-03.md), [first expansion](source-expansion-2026-10-20.md), [agency expansion](source-expansion-agencies-2026-10-20.md) or [INGO expansion](source-expansion-ingos-2026-10-20.md). A listed or historical advert is not necessarily open today.

## Strong integration candidates

| **Islamic Relief Worldwide, BiH representation** | The local organization published a **Child Welfare Officer** vacancy on 3 June 2026, based in **Tuzla**, restricted to BiH citizens, closing 11 June. The same notice confirms offices in Sarajevo and Tuzla. [Vacancy](https://islamic-relief.ba/zaposljavamo-sluzbenik-za-dobrobit-djece-child-welfare-officer/) | **Integrated.** The official [news listing](https://islamic-relief.ba/news/) carries the job alongside procurement, fundraising and ordinary news. Its public WordPress endpoint `https://islamic-relief.ba/wp-json/wp/v2/posts` returned HTTP 200 and `X-WP-Total: 192` on 20 October. Category `138` (news) contains 22 posts in 2026 and the paid vacancy; the adapter paginates and selects hiring titles. A live parser run found the Tuzla notice, its 3 June publication and 11 June deadline, and correctly treated it as expired. |
| **Goethe-Institut Sarajevo** | Its [local careers page](https://www.goethe.de/ins/ba/de/ueb/aus.html) links a full-time German-language teaching vacancy; the [notice](https://www.goethe.de/ins/ba/de/ueb/aus/sa.html) says remuneration follows its BiH pay scheme and applications are restricted to BiH citizens. | **Registered but disabled.** The first-party Sarajevo careers page has a distinct jobs block, and a local parser run found the teaching notice. It has no visible publication or closing date. The production server received HTTP 403 for the Bosnian, German and mobile pages despite Chrome TLS impersonation; the sitemap is reachable but not the vacancy details. The source was disabled in production on 20 October until the employer's site permits retrieval or offers another first-party feed. |
| **British Council BiH** | Its [BiH jobs page](https://www.britishcouncil.ba/en/about/job-opportunities) says specific roles are advertised as needed and directs staff applicants to the [global careers board](https://careers.britishcouncil.org/careers/search). Its own [education and society listing](https://www.britishcouncil.ba/en/programmes/education-society) also contains BiH-specific paid individual consultant calls, including a [2025–26 project lead based in BiH](https://www.britishcouncil.ba/en/programmes/education-society/call-counsultant-project-lead). | **High priority for consultancy calls, medium for staff.** The local listing is server-rendered and has several individual calls mixed with grants, participant opportunities and ordinary articles; parse notice/TOR dates and work location. Global Eightfold jobs require a separate country-filtered integration; no current BiH staff job was verified. Do not ingest the evergreen examiner invitation as a dated vacancy. |
| **Czech Embassy Sarajevo** | A [30 September 2025 embassy advert](https://mzv.gov.cz/sarajevo/bs/vijesti_arhiv/X2025_09_30_hoces_da_radis_kod_nas_ili_znas_nekoga.html) offered a full-time, indefinite-term development-cooperation coordinator post in central Sarajevo, restricted to BiH citizens, with a 6 October deadline. Crawl current articles, identify explicit local hiring and suppress posts after a closure notice or deadline. Its [RSS page](https://mzv.gov.cz/sarajevo/bs/rss.html) says no channels are active, so use HTML. |

## Defer or avoid as a BiH vacancy source

| Candidate | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| **Institut français de BiH** | A genuine first-party [Sarajevo French-teacher paid-hours advert](https://institutfrancais.ba/fr/cours-offre-demploi-professeur-de-francais/) exists, but its deadline was **3 March 2022**; a more recent [recruitment page](https://institutfrancais.ba/fr/recrutement-volontaires-%E2%83%92-sarajevo/) sought volunteers, not paid employees. The public WordPress posts endpoint returned HTTP 200 and `X-WP-Total: 722`, but there is no demonstrated recent paid local hiring. **Defer** until a fresh individual paid post appears; avoid broad ingestion of mixed cultural/grant posts. |
| **ICRC and IFRC** | The [ICRC's current BiH page](https://www.icrc.org/en/where-we-work/bosnia-and-herzegovina) lists its Sarajevo office, and its [careers board](https://careers.icrc.org/go/All-Jobs/3807301/) has country/location fields. The [IFRC's recruitment process](https://www.ifrc.org/get-involved/work-us/our-recruitment-process) says all vacancies use its [jobs portal](https://careers.ifrc.org/lumesse_jobsearch.html); an [IFRC plan](https://adore.ifrc.org/Download.aspx?FileId=611555) lists a Sarajevo cluster office. No BiH job in the inspected current listings was established. **Watchlist:** technically usable global portals, but check for a BiH duty-station role before adding an active BiH source. |
| **Transport Community, CEFTA, Energy Community** | These are real [Transport Community](https://www.transport-community.org/job-opportunities/), [CEFTA](https://cefta.int/vacancies/) and [Energy Community](https://www.energy-community.org/secretariat/jobs.html) vacancy boards. Their secretariats and example staff jobs are based respectively in **Belgrade** ([Transport Community regulations](https://www.transport-community.org/staff-regulations/)), **Brussels** ([CEFTA vacancy](https://cefta.int/vacancies/vacancy-announcement-trade-expert-trade-in-services/)) and **Vienna** ([Energy Community jobs](https://www.energy-community.org/secretariat/jobs.html)). **Avoid as local BiH staff feeds**; reconsider only a notice explicitly permitting a BiH work location or an individual assignment in BiH. CEFTA's rolling [expert database](https://cefta.int/vacancies/join-the-cefta-expert-database/) is not a specific open job. |
| **JICA Balkan Office** | The [current JICA office directory](https://www.jica.go.jp/english/about/basic/structure/overseas/balkan/) gives **Belgrade, Serbia** as the Balkan Office address while confirming coverage of BiH. This conflicts with the older Sarajevo-address statement in [the 3 October employer note](ngo-donor-employers-2026-10-03.md). **Avoid as a Sarajevo office source** until a current first-party local office or BiH work-location notice is found. |
| **Other proposed embassies, ADA, KfW, Helvetas, CARE, World Vision, PIN, Caritas, Hilfswerk** | Already evaluated with direct sources in the linked audits and expansion notes. In particular, an embassy's physical presence does not establish a public vacancy feed; the registry records no verified local recruitment list for the proposed Austria/France/Netherlands/Norway/Poland/Hungary/Croatia/Serbia/China/Saudi/Qatar/Slovakia candidates. KfW and several INGOs have separate recommendations in the expansion notes. Avoid duplicate sources for the same employer. |

**Implementation status:** Islamic Relief BiH is enabled and its first production scrape succeeded. Goethe-Institut Sarajevo is recorded but disabled because the production host is blocked. British Council BiH local consultancy calls and the Czech Embassy archive remain researched candidates; both mix vacancies with other notices, and the British Council's project-lead deadline is in a linked PDF rather than the HTML detail. For each future integration, retain a candidate only when its notice identifies an individual paid role, a BiH duty station or explicit BiH eligibility, and a still-valid application window. Where a deadline is absent, report status as unknown rather than open.
